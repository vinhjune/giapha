Same sample family tree, three visual directions for the node/card style, connectors, and navbar. Tree layout/algorithm is unchanged — only the skin differs.

A — Clean Minimal

White cards, soft shadow, avatar-ring gender accent, indigo selection, slate connectors. Neutral and highly legible for dense trees.

B — Heritage Editorial

Warm parchment tones, maroon + gold accents (extends current red-700 brand), serif type, monogram avatars. Distinctive, ancestry-appropriate.

C — Vibrant Generational

Cards colored by generation depth rather than gender, so each tree row reads as a band. Best for parsing large/deep trees at a glance.
